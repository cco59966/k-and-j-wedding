Questions & Answers

Q&A

01

What time should I arrive for the ceremony?

Our wedding ceremony will begin promptly at 4:00pm at St. Mark's Episcopal Church in Basking Ridge. We recommend arriving 15-20 minutes early to allow plenty of time to find your seat and get settled before the ceremony begins.

02

Are children invited?

While we love your little ones, our wedding will be an adults-only celebration. We appreciate your understanding and hope you're able to enjoy a fun night out with us!

03

What is the dress code?

The dress code for our wedding is cocktail attire. Think cocktail dresses, dressy jumpsuits, suits, or a jacket and dress pants.

04

Will transportation be provided?

Yes! Transportation will be available throughout the day. On your RSVP card, please check off which transportation options you will need:

• Hotel to Church
• Church to reception
• Reception back to the hotel

You do not need to be staying at the hotel to use the provided transportation. Please feel free to reach out to us with any questions!
